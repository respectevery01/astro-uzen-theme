Spacing is invisible when it's right, and painful when it's wrong. Every misaligned element screams "amateur."

## The 4px baseline

Everything should be a multiple of 4 (or 8). This creates rhythm without rigidity.

## Don't mix systems

If your CSS uses `0.5rem` in one place and `7px` in another, you've already lost. Pick one system and commit.
