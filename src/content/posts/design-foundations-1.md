Your brand starts with type. Before anyone reads a word, they've already formed an opinion based on the fonts you chose.

## The 3-second test

Users decide if a site is trustworthy in 3 seconds. Typography does 80% of the heavy lifting in that window.

## System fonts are not enough

They're fast, sure. But they carry no personality. Your brand deserves better than `-apple-system`.

## Where to start

Pick a display serif for headlines. Pair it with a clean sans for body. That's 90% of the work.
